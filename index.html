One book calls every department.

Each connected tool becomes a department in the book. Skills turn a plain request into a rehearsed cue: list a board, draft the email, pull the template, then show you the line before it ships.

Skill: email:write_and_send

Email department

The email skill drafts in your voice, reviews before it touches an outbox, and learns from the drafts you keep. Drafts sit in your mail app until you say GO.

MCP · email — connected
  • Q-01Say what you need: “kickoff email to the design team, project timeline attached.”
  • Q-02The skill writes it to Gmail drafts, never sends unprompted.
  • Q-03You edit, then hit GO, or it stays a draft.

Skill: project:sync_overview

Projects department

Read and write Trello or Monday through MCP, with no copy-paste and no manual card surgery. The center keeps one running overview of what is due, who owns it, and what blocks it.

MCP · trello + monday · connected
  • Q-01“Pull this week’s cards from Launch, list them by status.”
  • Q-02Boards and lists update live through the MCP server.
  • Q-03“Create a card ‘Design review’ on Week 1” lands in Trello, assigned and dated.

Skill: newsletter:design_and_send

Design department

Pull an existing Canva template, edit the date and hero through the Canva MCP, preview, then hand the finished newsletter to the email department. One request, whole workflow.

MCP · canva · connected
  • Q-01“Design a newsletter from my ‘Q3 Update’ template.”
  • Q-02Template loads, fields edited in your brand, previewed.
  • Q-03Emailed via the email department, or saved as a draft first.
