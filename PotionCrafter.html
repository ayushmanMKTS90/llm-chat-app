How to Play

◽
Day — Walk (WASD) to creatures, press E to harvest ingredients. Creatures need time to regenerate! Enemies chase you — your wizard auto-fires at them!
◾
Night — Select ingredients via click or 1-0 keys, then click BREW. Match the customer's recipe to earn coins.
↻
Cauldron slots — Click a filled slot to return the ingredient. Both slots must hold different ingredients matching the recipe.
★
Tip — Ingredients are limited! Harvest wisely from all 5 creatures to stock up.
